# Hook examples

Every hook in `catalog/hooks.md` with its three examples, each from a different niche. The rules for writing each hook are in the catalog (`unfaced show hook <n>`).

The hook reel shows the first example of every hook, spoken, each over the title scene of the next style in turn. Make it with:

```bash
npm run unfaced -- reel hooks
```

It is written to `examples/hooks/hook-reel.mp4` (not kept in the repository; it is attached to each release).

## 01 Shocking question

- Animals: "Why do cats purr when they're hurt?"
- Space: "What would happen if you fell into Jupiter?"
- Food: "Why does honey never go off?"

## 02 Absurd number

- Nature: "Twenty quadrillion ants are alive right now."
- Money: "Someone once paid ten thousand bitcoin for two pizzas."
- Body: "Your blood vessels would wrap around the Earth twice."

## 03 You're doing it wrong

- Food: "You've been cutting avocados wrong your whole life."
- Tech: "You're charging your phone wrong."
- Health: "You're brushing your teeth at the wrong time."

## 04 Impossible image

- Nature: "This lake is pink, and nobody added anything to it."
- Engineering: "This bridge is held up by nothing but its own shape."
- Animals: "This frog has a see-through belly. Watch its heart beat."

## 05 Unfinished sentence

- Animals: "The real reason cats knock things off tables is..."
- History: "The Eiffel Tower was almost torn down because..."
- Psychology: "The first thing people notice about you is..."

## 06 Bold claim

- History: "Napoleon wasn't short."
- Science: "Glass is not a slow liquid."
- Animals: "Goldfish have better memories than you think."

## 07 Countdown tease

- Animals: "Five animals that cheat death, and number one is immortal."
- Tech: "Four gadgets you didn't know you needed. Wait for number one."
- Travel: "Three places you can't visit. Number one is in Europe."

## 08 POV

- History: "POV: you built the pyramids and got paid in beer."
- Jobs: "POV: you're the first person to taste coffee."
- Animals: "POV: you're a sloth and you need the bathroom."

## 09 Nobody talks about this

- Business: "Nobody talks about why every Apple ad shows nine forty-one."
- History: "Nobody talks about the war that lasted thirty-eight minutes."
- Health: "Nobody talks about what sitting all day does to your hips."

## 10 Before/after flash

- Engineering: "This started as a pile of sand." (over a glass skyscraper)
- Food: "This is what one coffee bean becomes."
- History: "This was a swamp two hundred years ago." (over a city)

## 11 Insider secret

- Jobs: "The pilot and co-pilot never eat the same meal."
- Food: "Chefs salt pasta water far more than you'd dare."
- Retail: "Supermarkets put milk at the back for a reason."

## 12 Challenge

- Puzzles: "You have three seconds to spot the odd one out."
- Language: "Bet you can't say this word without laughing."
- Science: "Guess which weighs more. Most people get it wrong."

## 13 Start at the climax

- History: "The ship is sinking, and the band keeps playing."
- Space: "Apollo 13, two hundred thousand miles from home. A tank explodes."
- Business: "The company has one day of money left."

## 14 Relatable pain

- Tech: "Your phone dies at twenty percent. Here's why."
- Home: "You walk into a room and forget why."
- Food: "The ketchup won't come out, then it all comes out."

## 15 Contrarian take

- Health: "Eight glasses of water a day? You don't need them."
- History: "Vikings never wore horned helmets."
- Productivity: "Waking up at five won't make you successful."
