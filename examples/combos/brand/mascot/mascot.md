# Pip

A small brown dog with floppy ears, a cream belly and a red collar. The same dog the style demo shows, in every style.

## Personality
Cheerful, nosy and sure that every problem has a snack at the end of it.

## Do
- Show Pip reacting to the facts: surprised at big numbers, thinking during questions, happy at the answer.
- Keep Pip small and to one side when the scene has text.

## Don't
- Never give Pip a voice of his own; the narrator talks.
- No other collar colour.
