## Hook
What if you jumped into a hole straight through the Earth?

## Body
Say you jump in from Spain. The far end of the tunnel comes out in New Zealand.

The tunnel has no air and no heat. Gravity pulls you down, faster and faster, and you float like an astronaut.

About nineteen minutes later, you race through the centre at kilometres per second.

Then gravity pulls you back. You slow down and stop, right at the edge in New Zealand.

Nobody grabs you, so you fall again, all the way back to Spain. Back and forth, forever.

## Ending
But Earth spins, so you would hit the wall first. Which wall? That's next.
