## Hook
What if you jumped into a hole straight through the Earth?

## Body
Say the tunnel has no air and no heat. Gravity pulls you down, faster and faster.

About nineteen minutes later, you race through the centre at kilometres per second.

Then gravity pulls you back. You slow down and stop, right at the other side.

Nobody grabs you, so you fall again. Back and forth, forever.

## Ending
But Earth spins, so you would hit the wall first. Which wall? That's next.
