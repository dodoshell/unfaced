## Hook
Pip is halfway up the fridge. The cookie jar is wobbling.

## Body
Pip is a small brown dog, and he wants one thing: that jar.

Every morning his owner lifts it higher, laces her running shoes, and leaves.

So Pip stacks a cushion, a chair and the laundry basket, and climbs.

The jar is empty. The cookies left an hour ago, in her running pocket.
