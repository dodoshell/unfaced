## Hook
The engines drone for hours. Then you switch on your headphones, and the roar vanishes.

## Body
A tiny microphone on the outside of each ear cup listens to the noise around you.

A chip flips that sound wave upside down, making its exact opposite, called anti-noise.

The speaker plays the anti-noise with your music. Each peak meets a dip, and they cancel out.

## Ending
So the roar never vanished. Your headphones drowned it in its own opposite.
