## Hook
Everyone knows goldfish forget everything in three seconds. They don't.

## Body
Myth. Goldfish can remember things for months.

In one experiment, goldfish learned to push a lever for food, and only during one set hour of the day.

They can also learn to tell shapes, colours and sounds apart.

Nobody knows who said three seconds first. A fish circling its bowl just looks forgetful.

## Ending
So if your goldfish looks bored, it probably remembers yesterday.
