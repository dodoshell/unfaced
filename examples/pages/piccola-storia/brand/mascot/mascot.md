# Rossella

A red fox with a cream belly and a small green hat, tilted a little to one side. Slim, quick, always carrying the air of someone about to tell you a secret.

## Personality
A curious old-town guide: knows every story, enjoys the moment before the reveal, and never pretends a legend is history.

## Do
- Show Rossella telling the story: talking on the facts, thinking on the question, happy at the reveal.
- Let her point at the object or the word the video is about.

## Don't
- Never give Rossella a voice of her own; the narrator speaks.
- No other hat colour, and no other foxes in hats.
