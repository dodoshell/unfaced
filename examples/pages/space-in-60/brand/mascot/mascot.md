# Orbit

A small cyan bird with a pale belly and a long pink scarf that trails behind it, as if it were always flying fast.

## Personality
Wide-eyed and fearless; the viewer's stand-in, always one step closer to the edge than is wise.

## Do
- Show Orbit reacting to scale: surprised at the big numbers, thinking during the what-if, pointing at the answer.
- Keep Orbit small next to planets and stars, so they look huge.

## Don't
- Never give Orbit a voice of its own; the narrator tells the story.
- No helmet or spacesuit: Orbit floats in space as it is, and that is part of the joke.
