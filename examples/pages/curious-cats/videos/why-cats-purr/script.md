## Hook
Why do cats purr?
> visual: a cat on a sofa, eyes half closed

## Body
Because they're happy. Everyone knows that.

Actually, that's only half the story.

Cats also purr when they're scared, when they're hurt, and even while giving birth.

Purring seems to calm them down, like humming to yourself in a scary place. Some scientists even think the low vibrations help their bones and muscles heal.

And some cats hide a tiny cry inside the purr, one that sounds a lot like a human baby. People find it very hard to ignore.

## Ending
When your cat purrs at five in the morning, it isn't love. It's breakfast.
