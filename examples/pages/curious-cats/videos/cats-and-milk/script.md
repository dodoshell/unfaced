## Hook
Still giving your cat milk? You're doing it wrong.
> visual: a cat staring at a saucer of milk

## Body
Most adult cats can't digest milk properly. For many of them, one bowl means an upset stomach a few hours later.
> visual: a cat holding its belly

Kittens make an enzyme called lactase to digest their mother's milk. But most cats lose it as they grow up.
> visual: a kitten growing into an adult cat

And here's the strangest part. Cats can't taste sweetness at all. Their sweet taste gene is broken, so the sugar in milk means nothing to them.
> visual: a cat ignoring a cupcake

## Ending
Give them water instead. Your carpet will thank you.
