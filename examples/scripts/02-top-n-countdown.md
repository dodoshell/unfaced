## Hook
Earth's three biggest deserts. Number one is covered in ice.
> visual: sand dunes fading into a white ice sheet

## Body
The Sahara, across North Africa. At about nine million square kilometres, it's the biggest hot desert in the world.
> visual: endless orange dunes under a hard sun

The Arctic. Deserts are about how little rain or snow falls, not heat. The frozen north counts, at almost fourteen million square kilometres.
> visual: a flat white plain under a grey sky

And number one: Antarctica. Over fourteen million square kilometres of ice, on the driest continent on Earth.
> visual: a penguin crossing an endless ice desert

## Ending
Which would you rather cross: the Sahara or Antarctica?
