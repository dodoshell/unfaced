## Hook
Over a million times Apollo 11's memory, in your pocket.
> visual: a phone next to a bulky grey box from 1969

## Body
Memory. A phone: several gigabytes. Apollo's computer: about 4 kilobytes. Phone wins.
> visual: an ocean next to a tiny bucket

Speed. A phone: over 2 gigahertz, on several cores. Apollo's computer: about 2 megahertz. Phone again.
> visual: a spinning jet engine next to a bicycle wheel

Weight. A phone: under 200 grams. Apollo's computer: 32 kilograms. Phone sweeps it.
> visual: the phone on a scale, then the grey box on the same scale

The verdict: your phone wins every round, but Apollo's computer helped land people on the Moon.
> visual: a lunar module touching down

## Ending
Would you trust your phone to fly you to the Moon?
