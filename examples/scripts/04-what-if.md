## Hook
What would happen if the Sun vanished right now?
> visual: a sunny city street, a timer starting at 0:00

## Body
For eight minutes and twenty seconds, nothing. The Sun's last light is still on its way to us.
> visual: a beam of light racing from an empty spot towards Earth

Gravity travels at light speed too. So for those eight minutes, Earth keeps circling an empty spot, then flies off in a straight line.
> visual: Earth leaving its round orbit along a straight arrow

Now Earth is in the dark. Plants stop making food from sunlight, and the planet gets colder every day.
> visual: frost spreading over a dark planet

## Ending
But some living things wouldn't even notice. That's next.
