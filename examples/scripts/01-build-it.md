## Hook
This bridge is just stones, with nothing holding them together.
> visual: an old stone arch bridge over a river, no mortar between the stones

## Body
The easy way: one long stone slab across the river. Load it, and it snaps. Stone hates bending.
> visual: a stone slab sagging and cracking under a cart

So cut wedges and set them in a curve. Every stone now squeezes its neighbours, and stone loves being squeezed.
> visual: wedge-shaped stones in a curve, arrows pressing along it

Half an arch falls, so builders rest the wedges on a wooden frame, add the top stone last, then remove the frame.
> visual: the keystone sliding into place, then the wooden frame lowered away

## Ending
Nothing holds those stones together. They hold each other.
