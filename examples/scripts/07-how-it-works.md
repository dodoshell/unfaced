## Hook
Your phone ignores you the moment you put gloves on.
> visual: a gloved finger tapping a phone, nothing happening

## Body
A see-through layer under the glass holds a tiny electric field.
> visual: a glowing layer appearing under the phone screen

Your skin conducts electricity. When your finger comes close, it changes the field at that spot.
> visual: a ripple in the glowing layer under a fingertip

The phone reads where the field changed, and that spot is your tap.
> visual: a crosshair locking onto the fingertip

Most gloves don't conduct, so the field never changes. Touchscreen gloves hide conductive threads in the fingertips.
> visual: a glove fingertip with shiny threads woven in

## Ending
Your phone doesn't feel your finger. It feels your electricity.
