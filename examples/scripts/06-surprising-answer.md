## Hook
Bet you can't name the planet closest to Earth.
> visual: the planets lined up, a timer counting down from three

## Body
Venus. It's right next door.

Only sometimes.
> visual: a question mark over Venus

On average, the planet closest to Earth is Mercury. It's the closest to every other planet, too.
> visual: Mercury glowing next to the Sun

Venus can drift about 260 million kilometres away, on the far side of the Sun. Mercury hugs the Sun, so it never strays that far from us.
> visual: Venus swinging wide behind the Sun while Mercury circles close in

## Ending
So which planet is closest to Earth? On average, the smallest one.
