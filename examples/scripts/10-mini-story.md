## Hook
POV: you're the only cloud that can't rain.
> visual: a small white cloud among big grey rain clouds

## Body
You're Puff. The other clouds rain on the town. You drift, small and white, over a hot beach.
> visual: dark clouds pouring on rooftops, one little cloud over the sand

The big clouds rumble: a cloud that can't rain is just fog with dreams.
> visual: storm clouds laughing with flashes of lightning

You puff up. You hold your breath. You think heavy, grey thoughts. Not one drop.
> visual: the little cloud straining, cheeks puffed, still white

Then you see a girl on the sand, reading in the only shade on the beach. Yours.
> visual: a girl in the cloud's shadow, looking up and smiling

## Ending
In part two: a storm needs your help.
