## Hook
Banana farmers never plant a single seed.
> visual: a banana farmer holding a shoot instead of a seed

## Body
A banana is a berry, botanically speaking. A strawberry isn't.
> visual: a banana stepping into a "berry" box while a strawberry is turned away

The bananas in shops have no real seeds. So every new plant is grown from a piece of an old one, a clone.
> visual: a shoot cut from the base of a banana plant and replanted

Most bananas shipped around the world are one variety, the Cavendish. And clones share weaknesses: in the 1950s, a fungus ruined the banana before it, the Gros Michel.
> visual: rows of identical bananas, then a field of wilting plants

## Ending
So your banana is a copy of a copy of a copy.
