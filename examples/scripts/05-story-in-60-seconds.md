## Hook
A thief made the Mona Lisa a superstar.
> visual: the Mona Lisa with a burglar's shadow falling across her

## Body
August 1911, Paris. A former Louvre worker, Vincenzo Peruggia, walks into the closed museum in a staff smock.
> visual: a man in a white smock crossing an empty gallery

He takes the painting off the wall, leaves the frame on a stairway, and slips out.
> visual: an empty frame lying on a staircase

Nobody notices until the next day. Then she's in newspapers everywhere, and crowds stare at the empty wall.
> visual: newspapers spinning, then a crowd facing a bare wall

Two years later, he's caught in Florence. She goes home more famous than ever.
> visual: a wooden trunk opening to show the painting
