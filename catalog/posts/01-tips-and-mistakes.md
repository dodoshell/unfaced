# 01 Tips and mistakes

## What it's for

Teach one useful thing as a short list of mistakes to avoid or tips to use. Each slide holds one point, so the reader gets value on every swipe and keeps going to see the rest. It suits advice, habits and "what most people get wrong" posts.

## Slides

`cover → point (one per tip) → stat (optional) → cta`

- `cover`: the promise, with the number of points in it ("5 mistakes that...").
- `point`: one tip or mistake per slide. The headline names it; the body says why it matters and what to do instead, in 45 words or fewer.
- `stat` (optional): a figure or fact where it makes a point land, placed after the point it backs.
- `cta`: one invite, such as saving the post or asking a question.

For a short post use three points; for the longest use six. A `list` slide can replace the points when each is only a few words.

## Cover

The headline promises a number and a gain or a loss, in 12 words or fewer, with the key word as the accent.

- "5 mistakes that *slow down* your small shop"
- "Stop doing *this* when you write a quote"
- "3 habits that make a *tidy* desk stay tidy"

## Writing rules

- One idea per slide. If a point needs two sentences of "and also", split it.
- The headline of each point is the tip or the mistake itself, not a teaser. The reader should get it without reading the body.
- Say what to do instead, not only what is wrong.
- Number the points in the cover and keep that number: count the point slides.
- A stat or fact must be true and checkable; leave it out if you cannot source it.
- End on one action the reader can take today.

## Example

```markdown
---
title: Three mistakes in a first shop
post_type: 1
label: SMALL SHOP TIPS
---

## cover
# 3 mistakes that *slow down* a new shop
Studio Nord has seen all of them.

## point
# Photos taken in a *hurry*
Dark, tilted photos make good products look cheap. Use a window, a white sheet and your phone held level. It takes ten minutes.

## point
# No *return* policy on show
Buyers look for it before they pay. Put two plain sentences on the product page and at the checkout.

## point
# Prices that *change* weekly
Regular customers notice. Pick a price, keep it for a season, and use a fixed sale date when you want to discount.

## stat
# 70%
of shoppers read the returns page before buying.

## cta
# Save this *for your next listing*
Follow @studionord for one shop tip a week.
[Save this post]
```
