# 03 About us

## What it's for

Introduce who you are and why anyone should follow or hire you: who you are, what you do, why you do it, the proof, and how to start. It is the post a new follower reads first, so it answers "what is this page and is it for me?".

## Slides

`cover → point → list|steps → compare → stat|quote → cta`

- `cover`: who you are, in one line a stranger understands.
- `point`: what you do and for whom, in plain words.
- `list` or `steps`: what you offer (a `list`) or how you work (`steps`). Choose one.
- `compare`: your way against the usual way, in at most four items per side.
- `stat` or `quote`: the proof, one number or one customer's words.
- `cta`: the next step, such as following, joining a list or sending a message.

For a short post drop the `compare`. Keep the order: who, what, why, proof, how to start.

## Cover

The headline says what you are and one thing that makes you different, in 12 words or fewer, with the difference as the accent.

- "We are Studio Nord: *posters* for small shops"
- "Hi, we make *prints* by hand and by chance"
- "A tiny studio with a *big* press"

## Writing rules

- Start with what the reader gets from you, then who you are. People follow benefits, not résumés.
- Concrete beats clever: a place, a number, a thing you made.
- The comparison is about method or values, never about naming or insulting competitors.
- The proof must be real: a count, a year, a customer's own words with their first name or role.
- No jargon and no list of everything you do; pick three things at most.
- Write "we" or "I" consistently, matching the page's tone.

## Example

```markdown
---
title: Who is behind Studio Nord
post_type: 3
label: ABOUT US
---

## cover
# We are Studio Nord: *posters* for small shops
Two people, one press, a lot of ink.

## point
# We help local shops *stand out*
A bakery, a bike repair, a bookshop: we design and print the posters that make people stop and walk in.

## list
# What we *make*
- Window posters
- Menu boards
- Opening-day flyers
- Stickers and labels

## compare
# Our way vs the *usual* way
- ✗ Templates everyone uses
- ✗ Weeks of waiting
- ✗ Files sent back and forth
- ✓ One design per shop
- ✓ Proof in two days
- ✓ One person to talk to

## stat
# 120
shops we have printed for since 2021

## cta
# Want a poster *with your name* on it?
Send us a message and tell us about your shop.
[Message @studionord]
```
