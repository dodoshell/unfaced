# 02 How it works

## What it's for

Explain a process or a concept in steps, so the reader leaves able to say "so that's how it works". Use it for a service your page offers, a method, or an everyday thing seen from the inside.

## Slides

`cover → point (the idea) → steps → point (the detail that matters) → stat or quote (optional) → cta`

- `cover`: the question or the thing, and the promise to explain it.
- `point`: the idea in one plain sentence, before any detail.
- `steps`: the whole process on one slide, 2 to 6 steps, each three words or fewer with an optional second line (`step | who or what does it`).
- `point`: the one detail a reader would get wrong or want to know more about. Add more `point` slides to go deeper into single steps.
- `stat` or `quote` (optional): a result or a customer's words.
- `cta`: how to try it or ask.

For a short post, drop the second `point` and the optional slide.

## Cover

Name the thing and promise clarity: a question, or "how X really works", in 12 words or fewer, with the thing as the accent.

- "How does a *custom order* really work?"
- "From idea to *finished print* in four steps"
- "What happens after you press *send*"

## Writing rules

- Explain one process. Its steps run in the order they happen, from what the reader does to what they get.
- Each step starts with the part that acts: a person, a tool or a place.
- At most one technical word per slide, explained where it first appears.
- The `steps` slide is the map; the `point` slides around it carry the explanation. Do not repeat the step text word for word.
- Say what physically or practically happens, not what something is "designed to" do.
- Show who does each step (you, us, the software) in the second line when it matters.

## Example

```markdown
---
title: How a custom print order works
post_type: 2
label: HOW IT WORKS
---

## cover
# How a *custom order* really works
Four steps from your idea to a box at your door.

## point
# You bring the idea, we handle the *rest*
Send a photo, a sketch or a sentence. Nobody needs to know file formats or colour codes.

## steps
# From message to *box*
- Brief | you
- Proof | us
- Print | our press
- Delivery | courier

## point
# The *proof* is the safety net
Before anything is printed you get a picture of the final result. If a colour or a word is off, we fix it for free, and only then we print.

## quote
# "I sent a pencil sketch on Monday and held the poster on Friday."
Giulia, Studio Nord customer

## cta
# Ready to *send yours*?
Tell us what you have in mind. Reply in our DMs, no forms to fill.
[Message @studionord]
```
