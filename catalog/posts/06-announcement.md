# 06 Announcement

## What it's for

Announce something new or an offer: a launch, a new service, a sale or an event. The reader learns what it is, why it matters to them, what the condition or the deadline is, and what to do. It works when the news is clear in the first slide.

## Slides

`cover → point (what is new) → list (what you get) → stat (the number or the date) → point (the condition) → cta`

- `cover`: the news in one line, with the date or the offer when there is one.
- `point`: what it is and why it matters to the reader, in 45 words or fewer.
- `list`: up to five things included or changed. Use `compare` instead for "old way, new way".
- `stat`: the number that matters most: the discount, the price, the date or the number of places.
- `point`: the condition or the deadline, stated plainly.
- `cta`: one action, such as booking, buying or joining a list.

For a short post drop the `list`. Use `image` for a photo of the product or the venue.

## Cover

The headline is the news, with the date or the key word as the accent, in 12 words or fewer.

- "New: *poster packs* for shop openings"
- "Spring sale: *20% off* until Sunday"
- "We are opening a *second* studio"

## Writing rules

- Say what is new in the first line. No teaser slides that hide the news.
- Say why it matters to the reader, not why you are excited.
- A deadline or a limit must be real and written with the date, in full, with the year when it could be unclear.
- State the conditions (who, how many, what is excluded) in the post, not only in the caption.
- One action at the end; do not offer three ways to respond.
- Do not announce things that are not decided yet.

## Example

```markdown
---
title: Opening-day poster packs
post_type: 6
label: NEW
---

## cover
# New: *poster packs* for shop openings
Everything a new shop needs on day one.

## point
# Open with a *full window*
Opening a shop is stressful enough. A pack gives you a window poster, a door sign and 100 flyers, designed together and delivered before you open.

## list
# What is in the *pack*
- One A2 window poster
- One door sign
- 100 flyers
- One free proof round

## stat
# 150
the pack price, delivery included

## point
# The *small print*
Available for shops opening before 31 March. Orders close two weeks before your opening date. Delivery in the same country only.

## cta
# Opening *soon*? Book your pack
[Message @studionord]
```
