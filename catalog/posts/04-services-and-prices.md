# 04 Services and prices

## What it's for

Show what you offer, who it is for, what it costs and how to ask for it, so a reader can decide without a call. It is a menu: clear options, honest starting prices and one way to get in touch.

## Slides

`cover → list (the services) → point (who it is for) → steps (how to order) → stat|quote (optional) → cta`

- `cover`: what you sell and "from" the lowest price, if you can state it.
- `list`: up to five services, with a price or range in the second line (`Poster A3 | from 40`).
- `point`: who it suits and who it does not, in 45 words or fewer.
- `steps`: how to order, three or four steps.
- `stat` or `quote` (optional): a delivery time, a count of jobs or a customer's words.
- `cta`: the one way to ask for a quote.

Add a `compare` slide to set two packages side by side (at most four lines per side).

## Cover

Say the offer and the entry price in 12 words or fewer, with the price or the offer as the accent.

- "Posters for your shop, *from 40*"
- "What we do and what it *costs*"
- "Three packages, *one* clear price list"

## Writing rules

- Prices are real and current. If prices vary, write "from" and say what changes them.
- One line per service: the name, then the price or range in the second line.
- Say who the offer is not for; it saves both sides time.
- Mention what is included and what is extra in the same slide as the price.
- Keep to five services or fewer; group the rest under "and more on request".
- Finish with exactly one way to get in touch.

## Example

```markdown
---
title: Studio Nord services and prices
post_type: 4
label: PRICES
---

## cover
# Posters for your shop, *from 40*
What we make, what it costs, how to order.

## list
# What we *offer*
- Window poster A2 | from 60
- Menu board A3 | from 40
- Flyer pack, 100 | from 35
- Sticker sheet | from 25

## point
# Who it is *for*
Small local shops that want a design made for them. If you need thousands of copies or a full brand identity, we will say so and point you to someone who does it better.

## steps
# How to *order*
- Message | you
- Quote | us, in a day
- Proof | you approve
- Delivery | in a week

## stat
# 7 days
from approved proof to a parcel at your door

## cta
# Get a *quote* today
Tell us the size and how many you need.
[Message @studionord]
```
