# Durations

Target lengths for a video. A page sets `duration` in seconds (`30`) or as a range (`30-45`); these entries are the presets the agent recommends from. The real length follows the voice: the video ends half a second after the last word (a fifth of a second for ending `01 Seamless loop`), and `unfaced make` warns when the voice-over falls outside the page's range.

Pacing rules for every length:

- Count about 3 spoken words a second with the free voice, pauses included (voices 01 and 03 about 2.8, voice 06 about 3.2, voice 02 energetic about 3.7: give it more words). A text-only video (voice 08) is paced for reading, about 160 words a minute.
- The hook is spoken within the first 3 seconds, and the first frame already shows it.
- Change the picture every 3–5 seconds: a new beat, a new list item or a new visual. A body paragraph is a beat; keep each one to one or two sentences (8–20 words).
- Cut before you speed up. A script that runs long loses a beat, not its pauses.

| Duration | Words | Body beats | Script types that fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 s | 40–45 | 1–2 | did you know (09), surprising answer (06) |
| 30 s | 85–95 | 3–4 | most types |
| 45 s | 130–140 | 4–6 | top N countdown (02), myth or fact (03), story in 60 seconds (05) |
| 60 s | 175–185 | 6–8 | story in 60 seconds (05), X vs Y (08), build it (01) |

## 01 15 seconds

**Status:** ready

One fact and one punch. Hook in the first second, no warm-up. Best with the `01 Seamless loop` ending, so the replay counts as watch time.

- Words: about 40–45, in a hook, one or two body beats and an ending.
- Good for: single facts, loops, a series that posts every day.

## 02 30 seconds

**Status:** ready

The default. A hook, three beats and an ending.

- Words: about 85–95.
- Good for: most videos; the length the platforms push most for new pages.

## 03 45 seconds

**Status:** ready

Room for a short story, a top 5 or a "why" with three reasons.

- Words: about 130–140.
- Keep a new fact or a new item at least every 8 seconds; the middle is where viewers leave.

## 04 60 seconds

**Status:** ready

Full stories, countdowns and comparisons. Every 10 seconds needs a new visual or a new fact, or viewers leave.

- Words: about 175–185.
- Use it once a page has followers; a new page's long videos rarely get watched to the end.
