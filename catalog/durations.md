# Durations

Target lengths for a video. A page sets `duration` in seconds (`30`) or as a range (`30-45`); these entries are the presets the agent recommends from. The real length follows the voice: the video ends half a second after the last word.

## 01 15 seconds

**Status:** ready

One fact and one punch. Hook in the first second, no warm-up. Best with the `01 Seamless loop` ending.

- Words: about 35–40.

## 02 30 seconds

**Status:** ready

The default. A hook, three beats and an ending.

- Words: about 70–80.

## 03 45 seconds

**Status:** ready

Room for a short story, a top 5 or a "why" with three reasons.

- Words: about 105–115.

## 04 60 seconds

**Status:** ready

Full stories, countdowns and comparisons. Every 10 seconds needs a new visual or a new fact, or viewers leave.

- Words: about 140–150.
