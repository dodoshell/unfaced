# Endings

How a video closes. The ending is the last beat of every script type and decides whether people replay, comment or follow.

## 01 Seamless loop

**Status:** planned

The last line flows straight back into the first, so the video replays without a visible cut.

## 02 Cliffhanger

**Status:** planned

Stop just before the answer.

## 03 Comment question

**Status:** planned

Ask the viewer a question to answer in the comments.

## 04 Follow for part 2

**Status:** planned

Promise a continuation in the next video.

## 05 Punchline

**Status:** ready

One short line that lands the video: funny, surprising or both. It reframes what the viewer just learned rather than repeating it.

- Keep it under 15 words.
- Put it on its own scene with nothing else on screen but the caption.
- Example: "So a polar bear is a black bear in a very clever coat."

## 06 Callback to the hook

**Status:** planned

Answer or echo the opening line.
