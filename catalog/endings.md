# Endings

How a video closes. The ending is the last beat of every script type (the `## Ending` section of `script.md`) and decides whether people replay, comment or follow. The script's front matter names it (`ending: 3`).

Rules for every ending:

- One or two short sentences, under 15 words in all. The video should end within a second of the last word.
- Don't summarise the video. The ending adds a turn, a question or a promise.
- No "thanks for watching", no "like and subscribe". The platforms already show those buttons.

What the video shows: endings 02, 03, 04 and 06 put a wordless sign over the last scene (an arrow, a question bubble, a follow button with a 2, an arrow turning back), so it works in every language. A style that has room draws the sign in its own last scene; otherwise the engine draws it at the top of the frame, above the captions, and `unfaced check` makes sure nothing else is there. If the page has a mascot, it reacts to the ending (surprised, thinking, pointing).

## 01 Seamless loop

**Status:** ready

The last line flows straight back into the first, so the video replays without a visible cut. Replays count as watch time, so a loop that works lifts every other number.

- Write it: an ending line that the hook completes. Read the ending and then the hook out loud: they should sound like one sentence.
- Example: hook "Cats purr for a reason nobody expected." … ending "And that's exactly why…" → back to "Cats purr for a reason nobody expected."
- What the video does: the voice-over gets a shorter tail, the last frames fade into the first frame (which already shows the hook), and the music runs to the cut instead of fading out.
- Good for: short facts, 15–30 second videos, hook 05 unfinished sentence.
- Avoid: videos over 45 seconds; the viewer no longer remembers how it started.

## 02 Cliffhanger

**Status:** ready

Stop just before the answer, and make the next video the answer.

- Write it: raise the last question, then stop. The viewer must know exactly what they didn't get.
- Example: "But the strangest thing about the purr is where it comes from. That's next."
- Sign: an arrow with dots that run on.
- Good for: series, stories, mysteries.
- Avoid: a cliffhanger with no next video planned; make the follow-up before you post this one.

## 03 Comment question

**Status:** ready

Ask the viewer a question to answer in the comments. Comments tell the platform the video is worth showing.

- Write it: a question with a quick, easy answer that people will disagree on: a choice, a guess, a personal story in one word.
- Example: "Would you rather have a cat's night vision or its sense of smell?"
- Sign: a question bubble.
- Good for: opinions, comparisons (script 08), myth or fact (script 03).
- Avoid: questions with one right answer that the video already gave.

## 04 Follow for part 2

**Status:** ready

Promise a continuation in the next video, so viewers follow to see it.

- Write it: say what part 2 will show, specifically, in one sentence.
- Example: "In part two: the one cat that broke all these rules."
- Sign: a follow button with a 2.
- Good for: series, long stories, top N countdowns split in two.
- Avoid: promising a part 2 you won't post within a few days.

## 05 Punchline

**Status:** ready

One short line that lands the video: funny, surprising or both. It reframes what the viewer just learned rather than repeating it.

- Keep it under 15 words.
- Put it on its own scene with nothing else on screen but the caption.
- Example: "So a polar bear is a black bear in a very clever coat."
- Sign: none; the style's own last scene (an exclamation badge in style 03) carries it.
- Good for: facts, humour, animals.

## 06 Callback to the hook

**Status:** ready

Answer or echo the opening line, so the video feels complete.

- Write it: repeat the hook's key words with the answer in them. If the hook was a question, the ending is its shortest answer.
- Example: hook "Why do cats purr when they're hurt?" … ending "So when a hurt cat purrs, it's healing itself."
- Sign: an arrow turning back.
- Good for: questions, stories, hook 01 shocking question, hook 06 bold claim.
- Avoid: repeating the hook word for word with nothing added.
